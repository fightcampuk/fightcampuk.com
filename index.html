What We Do

Built By Fighters,
For Fighters

Fight Camp started with a simple idea born from a shared love of martial arts. Too many fighters wanted more time to spar, more rounds to drill combinations, and better access to coaching. So we built it.

Fighters from gyms across London come together at one venue for 3 hours of open mat time. Boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, wrestling, jiu jitsu. Whatever your style, you're welcome.

On top of the sparring, every attendee gets access to on-site mental performance coaching, sports therapy, and PT guidance. This isn't your average open mat.

Founder

Tanser Shinasi

Founder & Mental Performance Coach

Tanser has studied human behaviour for over 10 years and is currently completing a Doctorate in Clinical Psychology. With a keen interest in working with athletes, he has supported combat sports fighters and Premier League footballers on the mental side of performance.

His love for martial arts started young, sparked by watching Tyson vs Lewis as a kid, and has stayed with him ever since. At Fight Camp, Tanser runs 1-to-1 sessions with fighters on confidence, focus, nerves, and post-fight reflection, giving them tools they can take straight into their next session.

@tansershinasi
